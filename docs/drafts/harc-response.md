Dear Rhys (and HARC team),

Thank you so much for getting in touch and flagging this. You are completely right — David took those high-resolution scans of the 20 Catholic registers (including BT97, AD8, BW85, BW95, BY2) in February 2023 specifically on commission for Findmypast (PO-002423), and they should never have been made available in any public preview.

To explain what happened: when setting up the initial web gallery demo for the David Lovelace Archive, an automated script was used to generate a sample of images from the unindexed archive drive. Because David’s camera scans were uncompressed 45 MB TIF files, the size-weighted sampling algorithm inadvertently selected nine of those register scans for the "uncategorized" demo bundle.

I have taken immediate action to resolve this:

1. Removed all Catholic register images and URLs from the live site, photo galleries, and manifests on bosci.net.
2. Taken down the release bundle containing those images.
3. Updated our access policy to ensure that History/CatholicRegisters/**, History/HRO/**, and all HARC custodial holdings are strictly classified as non-public and excluded from public search catalogs and future releases.
4. Audited our photo generation pipeline so restricted or commissioned archives can never be selected for sampling.

Thank you again for spotting this so promptly and letting me know — I really appreciate your vigilance and support as we work through cataloging David’s archive properly.

Best wishes,

Robin Lovelace
